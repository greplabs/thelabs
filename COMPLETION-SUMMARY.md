## Key Features Explained

### Why Freemium?
- ✅ Democratizes access to powerful software
- ✅ Builds trust through experience (not marketing)
- ✅ Sustainable growth through value-driven upgrades
- ✅ Free tier provides real value, not artificial limitations

### Why Core Open Source?
- ✅ **Trust**: Users can verify privacy and security claims
- ✅ **Security**: More eyes = faster bug discovery
- ✅ **Community**: Contributors improve code and become advocates
- ✅ **Innovation**: Ecosystem grows through forks and integrations
- ✅ **Business**: Reduces support costs, enables talent acquisition

### App Store Model
- ✅ Unified marketplace for discovery
- ✅ Direct funding through sponsorships
- ✅ Cross-promotion between products
- ✅ Unified billing and subscription management
- ✅ Community engagement and feedback
